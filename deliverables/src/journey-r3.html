New journey: prove the saving on past weeks before any live pilot

With 30% less budget, the pilot starts with a replay: what would a verified plan have cost on the same orders that were really shipped? Nothing changes in operations. Screens are from the running application; figures are synthetic cost units.

Who
A planner and the pilot sponsor in the Swedish pilot region (simulated), during the discovery weeks.
Goal
Know the real cost gap between how goods were actually allocated and a verified lowest-cost plan, and decide go or no-go for a live pilot.
Scenario
4 past weeks, each with the usual 8 files plus actual_plan.csv, the plan that was really shipped.

The decision rule

Go to a live pilot if the verified plan is at least 2% lower than the actual plan over the replayed weeks, with zero constraint violations. The 5% target from Round 1 stays the ambition, not the gate. The Pilot evidence page adds the weeks up and shows GO, NO-GO or "not decided" (fewer than 4 comparable weeks); a person decides.

Not measured by replay: planner time and on-time delivery. They move to the live pilot.

All four weeks are synthetic and the "actual" plan imitates a planner who follows habit: 3.16% shows the measurement works, it is not a forecast.
